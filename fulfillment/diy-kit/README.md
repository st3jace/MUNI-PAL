# Obligation Register — DIY kit

Free. Four files. Use them in this order.

| File | What it is | Hand it to |
|---|---|---|
| `CHECKLIST.md` | The complete-drop checklist. What "all the documents" means | Yourself, before you start |
| `SKILL.md` | A skill file for your AI assistant. It reads your closing documents and returns a candidate obligation map with clause citations, plus the questions for counsel | Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, or your agent tool. Paste it in, or attach it, then attach your documents |
| `REGISTER-TEMPLATE.md` | The three tables: register, items awaiting input, refusal log. Column names match the skill's output | Your spreadsheet |
| `LEGEND.md` | The four statuses, why there is no fifth, and the words that never appear | Your counsel and your board |

## The one rule

The skill produces **candidates**. A candidate becomes an obligation only when your bond counsel, municipal advisor, or dissemination agent approves the row, the clause, and the due dates, in writing. Dates are never computed from the document text by you or by the AI. That rule is the whole method. Everything else is filing.

## What this kit does not do

It does not get your counsel's approval, keep the calendar honest when the deal changes, bind evidence files to rows quarter after quarter, or keep itself working as models and prompts drift. Those are the jobs. If you would rather have them on your team than on your desk, that is the paid version: the 10-Day Obligation Register at muni-pal.io/obligation-register. Fixed fee.
