- [ ] **Post draft:**
  > Three things operators believe about tax-exempt bond finance that quietly cost them the deal:
  >
  > **Myth 1: "I need the financing team lined up before an issuer will talk to me."**
  > Backwards. A credible, documented readiness story is what *attracts* the underwriter and the issuer — the team assembles around a fundable file, not the other way around. Chasing a bank first is how operators stall for a year.
  >
  > **Myth 2: "This is only for groups that have issued bonds before."**
  > First-time operators close conduit deals routinely. The gate isn't a track record — it's whether *this* project's file can survive diligence. A dairy processor, a first-time housing developer, and a waste operator all cleared their *first* conduit deal this year.
  >
  > **Myth 3: "If the project is eligible, we're basically ready."**
  > Eligible ≠ ready. Between "qualifies" and "closes" sit the TEFRA hearing, volume-cap allocation (for private-activity deals), and a disclosure package that holds up — each with its own clock. Skipping that sequencing is the single most common reason a "ready" deal slides a quarter.
  >
  > If you've talked yourself out of a bond on any of these three — worth a second look. The real gap is usually months of specific work, not years of ineligibility.
