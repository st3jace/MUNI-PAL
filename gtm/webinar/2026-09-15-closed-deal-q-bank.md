# Closed-deal Q bank — workshop rehearsal

> **Deck alignment note — 2026-09-17:** This bank is the rehearsal source for slide 10. In the current audience-facing deck and script, say **record question** rather than **custody**, use the function-not-title framing rather than **clerk gap**, and explain the four statuses through traceable dates and files. Keep the fifth-status rationale only for a direct question about adding a fifth status.

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Use:** Dress rehearsals + live Q (min 24–27). Sort every question out loud before answering.
**Rails:** Never file · never say compliant / bond-ready · never compute deadlines · never materiality · never MA advice
**Status codes only:** `filed` · `not filed` · `evidence missing` · `not testable`
**Answer patterns (SCRIPT):**
- **Record / custody:** “In the register that would be a row under [doc], section [x], recipient [y], frequency [z]. Evidence that counts is [artifact] with delivery date. Row named OB-#, period, short name.”
- **Judgment / refuse:** “That is a question of whether it applies / whether it’s material / whether you’re late — that belongs with bond counsel or your dissemination agent. The register records the clause, logs your question with today’s date, and leaves the field empty until they answer in writing.”

## Warm-up (say before any answer)

“I’m going to sort that as a record, judgment, fit, or product question first — so we don’t accidentally invent an answer the register isn’t allowed to hold.”

---

### Q01 — What do we still owe after closing?
**Sort:** CUSTODY
**Spoken:** Closing ends issuance; it does not end undertakings. The register’s job is a labeled list of every reporting promise next to the clause that created it — annual, quarterly, event notices, the rest — using only what counsel or your DA approve in writing.
**Not:** “You’re behind” / “You’re fine” / any score.

### Q02 — Who owns the continuing-disclosure calendar after the deal team leaves?
**Sort:** CUSTODY
**Spoken:** The title varies, but the functions do not: someone must keep each promise visible, route the right record, and preserve the proof after the deal team leaves. Calendar entries in the register only come from **approved** dates — never computed from “180 days after FYE.”
**Not:** Naming yourself as the calendar owner for their org.

### Q03 — Can you point to the sentence that created each duty without three PDFs?
**Sort:** CUSTODY
**Spoken:** That’s the artifact. Every row is bound to document + section + quoted duty language. We transcribe; we don’t decide which clause controls. A candidate becomes an obligation only when your professional approves the row, clause, and dates in writing.
**Not:** Picking the controlling clause for them.

### Q04 — Are we compliant? / Are we bond-ready?
**Sort:** REFUSE
**Spoken:** We don’t use those words — ever. The register only carries four status observations about evidence vs approved dates. Compliance conclusions belong with counsel / DA / your board process. I’d rather refuse that than let a register quietly imply you’re fine.
**Not:** Soft yes, soft no, or “mostly.”

### Q05 — Are we late on the annual report?
**Sort:** REFUSE (observation only)
**Spoken:** What the register can say: if an approved date passed and there is no matching file in the vault you gave us, the row is **evidence missing**. That is not “late,” not “in default,” not a conclusion. Whether that means you are late is counsel / DA.
**Not:** “You’re late” or “you’re okay.”

### Q06 — Is a trustee change / rating change / insurance swap a material event?
**Sort:** REFUSE
**Spoken:** Materiality and event determination are judgment. We log the question, route to bond counsel or dissemination agent, leave the field empty until they answer in writing. That refusal is the product.
**Demo line:** Show refusal-log row with today’s date.

### Q07 — Which clause controls if the loan agreement and CDA conflict?
**Sort:** REFUSE
**Spoken:** Which clause controls is a legal call. We can show both candidate citations side by side, log the conflict as awaiting counsel input, and wait for written direction. We never pick a winner.
**Not:** “Usually the CDA wins…”

### Q08 — When is our next quarterly due if the indenture says 45 days after quarter-end?
**Sort:** REFUSE
**Spoken:** Computing a date from a formula is interpretation. We only calendar dates your professionals approve in writing. Until then the row can sit **not testable** or awaiting input — not a guessed date.
**Not:** Pulling out a calculator.

### Q09 — What do the four status codes mean?
**Sort:** CUSTODY
**Spoken:**
- **filed** — matching file in the vault for that period
- **not filed** — approved date still in the future
- **evidence missing** — approved date passed; no matching file in what you gave us
- **not testable** — no approved calendar date and no event supplied
The same four work across deals because each status comes from approved dates and matching files, not a score or an AI opinion.
**Not:** Color-coding risk / RAG scores.

### Q10 — Can we add a fifth status: “needs professional review”?
**Sort:** CUSTODY (product rule)
**Spoken:** Feels careful; it’s the opposite. The moment some rows get that flag, the ones that don’t read as cleared — without anyone writing “compliant.” Routing lives in awaiting-input and the refusal log, not in status.
**Not:** Agreeing to a custom fifth for “just our board.”

### Q11 — Does this replace our dissemination agent / bond counsel?
**Sort:** GATE / OFFER
**Spoken:** No. You or your dissemination agent file. Counsel and DA stay the judgment seat. We build the register from approved written inputs.
**Not:** “We’re cheaper than counsel.”

### Q12 — Is this for municipal issuers / our city / the authority?
**Sort:** GATE
**Spoken:** No path today for municipal entities or public authorities as buyers. Education only if they’re in the room. Buyers are private / nonprofit obligated persons with live post-close undertakings. The form will say so.
**Not:** Soft “maybe later” that sounds like a sales path.

### Q13 — We haven’t closed yet — can you help us get ready to issue?
**Sort:** GATE
**Spoken:** This product is post-close only, on purpose. Pre-issuance / readiness is a different job and we don’t sell it on this line. When the bonds have closed and undertakings are live, the gate opens.
**Not:** Bond Readiness Assessment language.

### Q14 — Our controller already tracks this in Excel — why pay?
**Sort:** CUSTODY / OFFER
**Spoken:** Great — this isn’t a parallel opinion engine. It’s the register they approve into: clause-bound rows, approved dates only, vault index, gap list, refusal log. A spreadsheet often depends on one person’s memory and maintenance. The paid register adds ongoing maintenance, documented follow-up for professional input, and a record that survives a staff change or refunding.
**Not:** Insulting their spreadsheet.

### Q15 — What can we try this week without sending you our binder?
**Sort:** OFFER
**Spoken:** Candidate Map in One Sitting — the free DIY instructions. Attach closing documents to an assistant you already use. It quotes deadline language verbatim and lists questions for counsel. It will not compute dates, assign status, judge materiality, or draft notices. A first pass is not ongoing recordkeeping — approvals and maintenance still need an owner.
**Not:** Promising the DIY map is “good enough forever.”

### Q16 — When is the done-for-you register worth a fixed fee?
**Sort:** OFFER
**Spoken:** When you want the maintained register: send the complete binder, professionals approve the list, and we build in ten business days from a complete document set; the clock pauses only for documented professional input. The fee is fixed after we confirm how many bond issues and disclosure documents are in scope — never a percentage of anything. We never file. Live workshop path: $2,500 if intake starts live or within 24 hours; otherwise $3,750. The exact quote still follows document review.
**Not:** Inventing scarcity unless hours are truly capped.

### Q17 — Will software / AI fill our deadlines automatically?
**Sort:** REFUSE
**Spoken:** No. Auto-filling from a formula is judgment wearing a spreadsheet. The AI can quote the language, but it does not approve rows, calculate dates, assign status, or answer judgment questions. Those durable limits reduce unsupported answers and make the output easier to check.
**Not:** “We’ll add AI deadline extraction next quarter.”

### Q18 — Can we color-code high-risk obligations for the board?
**Sort:** REFUSE
**Spoken:** That’s our assessment dressed as data. Boards get four status words and the supporting lists: gaps, awaiting input, and refusals. Risk ranking is counsel / management judgment.
**Not:** Offering a “risk heat map” as a custom.

### Q19 — We have multiple closed series and a refunding last year — how do you handle that?
**Sort:** CUSTODY
**Spoken:** Different series = different promise sets. Each undertaking stays bound to its series + clause. Refunding doesn’t erase what still lives — or what counsel says still lives. We don’t decide what survived; we wait for written direction and keep candidates in awaiting input until then.
**Not:** Declaring which series is “active.”

### Q20 — We’re a hospital / have PHI in reports — can you still build the register?
**Sort:** GATE / CUSTODY
**Spoken:** Sector doesn’t change the recordkeeping method — a private or nonprofit healthcare borrower can be in scope. PHI handling requires an operations, BAA, and counsel path before we receive sensitive documents. Status language stays the same; we don’t get a freer hand on judgment because the sector is regulated.
**Not:** Promising PHI processing without the hold path.
