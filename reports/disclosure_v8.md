## The Project
GSI's Caldor biomass project uses a 100 ton per day UCS system manufactured in GSI's facilities and deployed on an approximately 10 acre site near biomass collection and chipping operations to convert chipped forest biomass from salvage and fuel reduction efforts into diesel range fuels, biochar, electricity and heat.
The project is located in Wildfire-prone Western states CA, AZ, CO, NM, OR, WA, ID, MT, agricultural regions, and forest stewardship territories.
Current status: commercialization + pilot demonstration phase.
