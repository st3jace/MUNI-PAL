## The rule

> **No row, no claim.** Every public quantitative claim requires a CLAIMS-REGISTER row:
> claim → source artifact → provenance class → status. (LANGUAGE-POLICY §4.)

A claim not in this register may not appear on any public surface. A new claim enters
as PROPOSED and ships only after Arthur flips it to CERTIFIED.

## The two Section-B guardrails (binding on every row below)

> **1.** "Do not treat 'we can find a similar number in a Class C source' as re-derivation.
> Re-derivation means the *published number* is recomputed from the compliant source and
> restated at whatever value that computation actually produces. Matching a legacy number by
> selecting a source that happens to agree with it is reverse-engineering provenance, and it
> is worse than removal. If the restated value differs, publish the restated value."

> **2.** "Do not let the quarantine leak into internal analysis. The policy quarantines the
> legacy corpora **for new public claims**. Our internal understanding of those markets,
> built over months, is not deleted — it becomes *experience*, which is a legitimate and
> honestly-labeled basis for methodology-derived statements. The line is publication, not
> thought."

## Reading the table

- **Provenance class** per LANGUAGE-POLICY §3. The legacy 866-transaction healthcare corpus
  and the housing/waste corpora are **Class D** (collected via automated retrieval before the
  policy; quarantined for new public claims). "A" = our own deal record / own work product /
  own pricing. "C" = vetted licensed-free source. "UNCLASSIFIED" = terms not yet read — not
  usable until logged in the source register.
- **Disposition** applies Arthur's Section-B priors as the *starting* position:
  RE-DERIVE (recompute from A/B/C and publish the recomputed value) · RELABEL
  (keep the insight, present as methodology-derived, drop any implication of an observed
  market sample) · REMOVE · KEEP-AS-IS.
- **Status** is PROPOSED for every row in v0.1. Arthur flips rows to CERTIFIED or REJECTED.
- File paths are repo-relative to `MUNI-PAL/`. Frontend pages listed are post-scrub copy
  (EMMA/MSRB brand strings already removed per §1/§2).
