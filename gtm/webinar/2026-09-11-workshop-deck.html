3 · The method

Transcribe. Compare. Refuse.

Transcribe

Read every clause. Copy the promise and the deadline words exactly. Never reword a deadline.

Compare

Put each approved row next to the files you sent. Is there a file? Is it for the right period? That is the whole check.

Refuse

"Is it material?" "Are we late?" "Which clause controls?" We log it, send it to your lawyer, and leave it unanswered.

No AI makes a decision. Every step is a simple check we can run again and show your board.

4 · Four status words, no fifth

Four words. None of them is our opinion.

filednot filedevidence missingnot testable
  • filed: we have the file.
  • not filed: not due yet.
  • evidence missing: the due date passed, and there is no file.
  • not testable: no date to check against.

No score. No rating. No color by importance.

Why no fifth

"Needs a professional" feels careful. It is the opposite. It marks the rows we flagged, and the rows we did not flag now read as cleared. That is how "we track your promises" drifts into "you are fine" without anyone writing the word.
