3 · The method

Transcribe. Compare. Refuse.

Transcribe

Read every clause. Quote the duty and the deadline language word for word. Never paraphrase a deadline.

Compare

Put each approved row beside the files in the vault. Is there a file? Does it match the period? That is the whole test.

Refuse

"Is it material?" "Are we late?" "Which clause controls?" Logged, routed to your professional, left unanswered.

No model decides anything in this pipeline. Transcription and comparison are mechanical, so they can be tested and replayed.

4 · Four status codes, no fifth

The discipline is in what the vocabulary refuses to say.

filednot filedevidence missingnot testable

All four are observations about documents. Is there a file. Does it correspond. Can we test it.

No score. No rating. No colour by importance. No field that records our opinion of anything.

Why no fifth

"Needs a professional" feels careful. It is the opposite. It marks the rows we flagged, and the rows we did not flag now read as cleared. That is how "we track your obligations" drifts into "you are fine" without anyone writing the word.
