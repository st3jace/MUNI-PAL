# Adventist Pilot — Engagement Letter Language

**Status:** Draft for legal review
**Purpose:** Frame the instrumentation ask as a value exchange, not a research request. Make the "yes" easy.

> **Framing principle:** Adventist is not doing us a favor by being measured. We are doing them a favor by coordinating their next deal at no cost, and the measurement is how we both learn whether the service is worth continuing. The letter must read that way — value first, instrumentation second, never as a vendor extraction.

Thanks again for the conversation last week. As discussed, attached is the proposed engagement letter for the pilot on your upcoming [deal description].

Three things worth flagging up front:

1. **There is no fee for this engagement.** Launch Shop is absorbing the cost in exchange for the chance to work alongside your team on a real deal and learn what works.

2. **You retain full control.** Your existing underwriter, counsel, and MA relationships are unchanged. We sit alongside them, not in their seat. If at any point you want us out of the deal, we leave — no questions, no clawback.

3. **We are going to measure things.** Honestly, this is the part that matters most to us, and it's the part I want to be most transparent about. We will keep a log of what our system does on your deal, and we will ask you and your team for a 5-minute weekly note on time spent. At close, you get a written report comparing this deal to your prior comparable issuance — yours to use however you want, including not at all. The full instrumentation plan is in §3 of the attached letter.

If anything in here is uncomfortable, tell me directly and we'll fix it before signing. The worst outcome for both of us is a pilot that gets watered down to the point where neither side learns anything.

## Engagement letter (attachment)

### Muni-Pal Pilot Engagement — [Adventist Health Entity]

**Parties.** Launch Shop LLC ("Launch Shop"), operator of the Muni-Pal platform, and [Adventist Health entity] ("the Borrower").

**Subject deal.** [Description of upcoming issuance: par range, structure, expected timing.]

### 1. Scope of Services

Launch Shop will provide the Borrower with access to the Muni-Pal platform and direct hands-on support for the duration of the subject deal, including:

- Pre-issuance readiness assessment against the Muni-Pal 167-item framework, presented as observations against historical comparable deals rather than prescriptive recommendations
- Document organization, categorization, and information request routing between the Borrower and the Borrower's underwriter, counsel, and municipal advisor. Launch Shop will route requests and responses but will not draft or substantively edit the Borrower's responses.
- COI benchmarking based on historical comparable issuances, presented as statistical ranges derived from a dataset of prior healthcare deals, not as deal-specific cost projections or recommendations
- A dedicated point of contact at Launch Shop for the duration of the engagement

Launch Shop's role is to **support and accelerate** the Borrower's existing deal team, not to replace any member of it. Launch Shop will not provide advice on deal structure, terms, timing, sale method, choice of deal participants, credit enhancement, ratings strategy, or disclosure content. All final decisions on these matters remain with the Borrower and its existing registered advisors.

### 2. Fees

**No fee** is payable by the Borrower for the services described in §1. Launch Shop is providing these services at its own cost in exchange for the opportunity to participate in the deal and the measurement rights described in §3.

The Borrower remains responsible for all third-party costs of the issuance (underwriter discount, counsel, MA, rating agencies, etc.) on the same terms it would have absent this engagement.

### 3. Measurement and Data Rights

Launch Shop will collect the following during the engagement:

a. **Internal task log.** A record of tasks performed by the Muni-Pal platform on this deal, with timestamps and brief descriptions. Maintained by Launch Shop. Shared with the Borrower on request.

b. **Time diary.** A weekly 5-minute self-reported note from the Borrower's CFO and designated finance staff, indicating approximate hours spent on the deal during the prior week, in pre-defined hour buckets (under 10, 10–25, 25–50, 50–100, 100–250, 250+). The Borrower agrees to make a good-faith effort to provide this weekly diary. Missed weeks will be noted but will not be treated as breach.

c. **Milestone log.** Calendar dates of major deal milestones (engagement, POS, rating presentations, roadshow, pricing, close), captured as they occur.

d. **Closing COI data.** The final cost of issuance figures from the closing memo, line by line, captured at close.

e. **Post-deal interview.** A single 30-minute conversation with the CFO and (if available) one finance staff member, within 30 days of close, to capture qualitative observations.

The Borrower grants Launch Shop the right to use this data for: (i) improving the Muni-Pal platform and models, (ii) internal analysis, and (iii) producing the post-engagement report described in §4.

### 4. Post-Engagement Report

Within 30 days of close, Launch Shop will deliver to the Borrower a written report comparing this deal to the Borrower's most recent comparable issuance, including:

- Timeline comparison, milestone by milestone
- Estimated time savings by role, in hour buckets
- Predicted vs. actual COI, line by line
- Qualitative observations on what worked and what did not

This report is the Borrower's property. The Borrower may use it, share it, ignore it, or destroy it.

### 5. Confidentiality and External Use

a. **Borrower confidentiality.** Launch Shop will treat all non-public information shared by the Borrower as confidential and will not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or as necessary to perform the services in §1.

b. **Launch Shop external use.** Launch Shop will **not** publicly identify the Borrower or use the Borrower's name, deal details, or any quotation in marketing, sales, press, or investor communications **without the Borrower's written consent for each specific use**. Aggregated, anonymized data (e.g., "in our first instrumented engagement, the deal closed in X weeks") may be used without consent only if it cannot reasonably be traced to the Borrower.

c. **Reference rights.** Separately and at the Borrower's sole discretion, the Borrower may agree after close to serve as a written reference, accept introductions to peer institutions, or co-author a case study. None of these are obligations under this letter.

### 6. Termination

The Borrower may terminate this engagement at any time, for any reason or no reason, by written notice to Launch Shop. Upon termination:

- Launch Shop will cease all activity on the deal within 48 hours
- Launch Shop will return or destroy all Borrower confidential information at the Borrower's election
- Launch Shop retains the right to use only the data already collected under §3, subject to the confidentiality limits in §5
- No fee, penalty, or clawback is owed by either party

### 7. No Advisor Status; Independent MA Acknowledgment

Launch Shop is not a registered municipal advisor, broker-dealer, underwriter, or investment advisor. Nothing in this engagement is intended to constitute municipal advisory services as defined under Section 15B of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or related SEC and MSRB rules. The Borrower acknowledges that it is relying on its existing registered municipal advisor ("the Borrower's MA") and other registered professionals for all advice within the scope of those regulations.

**Condition precedent to effectiveness.** This engagement does not become effective until:

a. The Borrower has notified the Borrower's MA and lead underwriter in writing of Launch Shop's role under this engagement; AND

b. The Borrower has provided Launch Shop with a written acknowledgment from the Borrower's MA confirming that the MA is aware of Launch Shop's role, is providing municipal advisory services to the Borrower on the subject deal, and does not object to Launch Shop's scope of services as described in §1.

If either condition is not satisfied within 14 days of signature, either party may terminate this engagement at no cost.

> **[Legal review note:** §7 must be reviewed by muni-finance counsel before signing. Launch Shop's role coordinating information requests and document flow may or may not cross into regulated MA territory depending on how it is performed and whether the applicable exclusion path is IRMA (G-42) or the Section 15B(e)(4)(C) exclusion for unregistered persons. If there is any ambiguity, narrow the scope in §1 rather than broaden the disclaimer in §7.**]**

### 7A. Data Handling and Security

For the duration of this engagement, Launch Shop will:

a. Store all Borrower documents in an access-controlled workspace with encryption at rest and in transit.
b. Limit access to the workspace to Launch Shop personnel directly assigned to the engagement.
c. Not transmit Borrower documents to any third party except as required to route information requests under §1.
d. Upon termination or conclusion of the engagement, either return all Borrower documents to the Borrower or permanently delete them at the Borrower's election, within 30 days.
e. Make available, on request, a written description of its data handling practices sufficient for the Borrower's vendor security review.

The Borrower acknowledges that Launch Shop is not a HIPAA business associate and that no protected health information should be provided to Launch Shop under this engagement.

### 8. Term

This engagement begins on the signature date and ends on the earlier of: (i) 30 days after the close of the subject deal, (ii) termination under §6, or (iii) [date 6 months after expected kickoff].

## Internal notes (do NOT send)

**The hardest paragraph in this letter is §5(b).** It gives Adventist a per-use veto on every external mention. That feels expensive to us — we want to be able to say "we worked with Adventist" — but it is the right call:

1. It makes the "yes" radically easier. CFOs will sign things that protect their name. They will not sign things that put them in someone else's deck.
2. It forces us to come back and have a second conversation after close, which is exactly when reference rights become a real ask backed by real results.
3. If the pilot goes badly, §5(b) protects *us* too — we don't want to have to explain a public association that fizzled.

**The §7 disclaimer is a real legal exposure**, not a formality. The line between "platform that organizes documents" and "platform that gives municipal advice" is genuinely fuzzy when the platform is making recommendations about deal readiness, COI ranges, or timing. Before this letter goes out, Launch Shop counsel needs to actually read it and tell us whether the scope in §1 needs to be narrowed.

**What this letter does NOT include and probably should, eventually:**
- IP ownership of any custom artifacts produced for the Borrower
- Data security and handling commitments (where is the document drive hosted, who has access, retention, deletion)
- Insurance / indemnification — likely a deal-breaker for Adventist's vendor review if missing
- Any reference to the Borrower's underwriter and MA, who will need to be told this is happening and may have their own opinions

These are scoped out of v1 to keep the letter short enough to actually get read. Add them in v2 after legal review.
