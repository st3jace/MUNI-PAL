# Outreach Templates & Angle Bank

*Source scripts adapted from the Acquisition.com roadmap (Stages 1–2) for bond-finance BD. The daily agent personalizes from these; it never sends them verbatim.*

## Warm outreach (people who know you)

Skeleton (the "warm offer" script, adapted):

> {Name} — {genuine one-line personal reference from notes}. I've been deep in {bond readiness / facility finance} work — we just {specific recent result or build, from proof library}. I'm taking on a small number of {readiness engagements / platform pilots} and I'm giving the first conversations away free: a readiness walkthrough where you leave with {the gaps, the map, the next step} whether or not we work together. Know anyone with a facility project who keeps hearing "you're not bond-ready"? Happy to talk to them — or to you.

Rules: one personal line minimum, one proof point, ask for the referral OR the conversation, never both hard.

## Cold outreach (first touch, don't know you)

Skeleton — short, one insight, one question, no pitch:

> {Name} — saw {specific trigger: their project, filing, board agenda, post}. Most {operator type} get told they're 2 years from bond-ready when the real gap is usually {one specific, useful observation}. We built a system that maps exactly that for {comparable segment}. Worth a 20-minute look at where {org} actually stands?

Rules: the trigger must be real and named. The insight must be usable even if they never reply. Under 90 words.

## The hidden-cost interview (the free readiness conversation)

The two magic questions, verbatim:
1. **"What would it take?"** — after any hesitation or no; assumes the purchase, works backward into what the offer must contain.
2. **"If this were ${X} to get you to a closeable deal, what would you need to see to be excited to move forward?"** — price set first, surfaces the real objection.

Log every answer in `prospects.csv` notes — these are offer-design inputs (Value Equation: raise dream outcome & likelihood, cut time delay & effort).

## CLOSER call structure (when a conversation becomes a sales call)

**C**larify why they engaged → **L**abel the problem ("so you've got a fundable project but no readiness map — is that right?") → **O**verview past attempts ("what have you tried? how'd that go?") → **S**ell the vacation — the three-pillar pitch below → **E**xplain away concerns (time / stall / money / preference / authority) → **R**einforce the decision after the yes.

**Three-pillar pitch (bond readiness edition):** to get a facility funded you need (1) a *credible readiness story* (the facts lenders trust), (2) a *complete disclosure package* (the paper that survives scrutiny), (3) a *process operator* (someone who's run the gauntlet before). Most operators have one of three. We bring the other two. *(Analogy: a three-legged stool — any one missing and it tips.)*

## Content angle bank (rotate; one post/day)

1. **Deal anatomy** — walk one real deal stage (anonymized): what surprised the operator, what the timeline actually was.
2. **Readiness myth-busting** — "you need X before a bond" claims that are wrong, with the real threshold.
3. **Hidden-cost spotlight** — a non-obvious friction (disclosure prep hours, board cadence, info-request churn) and how to remove it.
4. **Proof story** — Epiphany Bridge format: where the client was → the objective numbers → why they chose this path. Only from `proof/index.md`.
5. **Checklist excerpt** — 3–5 items from the readiness checklist; give it away free.
6. **Market signal** — something from this week's EMMA / sector activity and what it means for operators.

Comment rule: the goal is the *best comment on the thread* — add a number, a counterexample, or a missing step. Engagement on the comment converts to a warm outreach the next day.

## Proof > promise checklist (apply to every draft)

Recent > old · specific numbers > adjectives · similar-to-prospect > generic · show > tell. If a draft makes a claim with no artifact behind it, cut the claim or swap in honest positioning ("we run this system live on our own engagements").
