## Content — 1 post (angle 1: deal anatomy — fresh cut, "the timeline nobody shows you")

*Longest-unused usable angle (last ran 07-01, deal-stage walkthrough). Fresh cut here = the *time distribution* across a real conduit deal rather than the stage list itself. Honest positioning; anonymized/composite; no client numbers (proof library empty).*

> **Here's where the time actually goes in a conduit bond deal — and it's not where operators brace for it.**
>
> Ask an operator what they dread about a facility financing and they'll point at the market: the pricing, the rate, the investor roadshow. That part is real — but in a typical conduit deal it's often the *shortest* leg. Here's the rough shape of where the calendar actually gets spent:
>
> **1. Eligibility & structure** — fast, once you know the conduit fits. Days to a couple weeks.
>
> **2. Readiness & disclosure prep** — the silent majority of the timeline. Assembling the paper that survives scrutiny, reconciling the numbers, building the story a lender can trust. This is where "we'll be ready in a few weeks" quietly becomes months — not because anything is wrong, but because nobody scoped it up front.
>
> **3. Issuer / board process** — paced by someone else's calendar (agendas, meeting cycles). Predictable if you respect it, expensive if you miss a cycle.
>
> **4. Market execution** — the part everyone fears is often the cleanest, *if* steps 2 and 3 were done right.
>
> The lesson operators keep learning the hard way: the risk isn't the market leg you're staring at. It's the disclosure leg you didn't budget for. Map that one first and the whole timeline stops surprising you.
>
> *(We built the system we use to scope step 2 before it becomes the bottleneck — and the first readiness conversation is free.)*
