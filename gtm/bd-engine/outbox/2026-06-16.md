> **"You need two years of stabilized operations before you can do a bond." — No, you don't. Here's what's actually true.**
>
> I hear some version of this every week from facility operators: a banker, a board member, or a well-meaning advisor tells them they're "years away" from tax-exempt bond financing. Usually it's wrong — or at least, it's measuring the wrong thing.
>
> Three myths worth retiring:
>
> **Myth 1: "You need to be big."** Conduit issuers route deals for small and mid-market operators constantly — single-facility dairy processors, recycling plants, regional housing developers. Size isn't the gate.
>
> **Myth 2: "You need years of seasoning."** What a credit committee actually needs is a *credible readiness story* and a *complete disclosure package* — both of which you build with preparation, not with time. An operator who prepares the paper can be closeable far sooner than one who just waits.
>
> **Myth 3: "A bank no means a bond no."** They're different lenders with different criteria. A bank declining your facility says almost nothing about whether a tax-exempt conduit path is open to you.
>
> The real gate isn't size or age — it's whether you can show up with the facts a lender trusts and the paper that survives scrutiny. That's a preparation problem, and preparation problems are solvable.
>
