## Content — 1 post (angle 5: checklist excerpt — "the 5-line readiness pre-check")

*Longest-unused usable angle (angle 5 has not run in the recent 06-29→07-08 window, which cycled 3→2→1). Give-it-away format: a short, genuinely usable checklist an operator can self-score against — the highest-trust content type because it's useful even if they never reply. Honest positioning; no client numbers (proof library empty); this is our own framework, not a claimed result.*

> **Before you spend a dollar on a bond financing, score yourself on these 5 lines. Most operators who get told "you're not ready" fail 2 of them — and both are fixable in weeks, not years.**
>
> A conduit bond isn't reserved for hospitals and universities. If you have a real facility project, the gate is usually readiness, not size. Here's the pre-check we run before anyone spends real money:
>
> **1. Three years of clean, reconciled financials** — not perfect, but consistent and explainable. Can you walk a stranger through a surprise line item without flinching?
>
> **2. A project story a lender can repeat** — what's being built, why the cash flow supports it, what happens if year one runs light. If your team can't say it in four sentences, it isn't ready to underwrite.
>
> **3. Named the conduit that fits** — the right issuer for your sector and geography. Most operators disqualify themselves here by assuming none exists. Usually one does.
>
> **4. A disclosure owner** — one person who owns the paper that survives scrutiny, before *and* after close (continuing disclosure trips up more repeat issuers than first-timers).
>
> **5. Someone who's run the gauntlet** — a process operator who's closed one before. This is the leg most projects are missing, and the cheapest to add.
>
> Score 3+ and you're closer than whoever told you "come back in two years" thinks. Score 2 or below and the gap is specific — not a wall, a punch-list.
>
> *(We built the system we use to run this pre-check on live engagements — the first readiness conversation is free, and you leave with your own punch-list either way.)*
