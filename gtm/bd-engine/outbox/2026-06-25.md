## Content (1 post) — Angle 5: Checklist Excerpt

**Topic: "The 5-point readiness check a lender runs before they fund your facility — give it to yourself first."** (Honest positioning — the checklist we run on live engagements; give it away free, no client numbers.)

> Operators ask me "how do I know if we're bond-ready?" like it's a mystery. It isn't. Lenders and underwriters run the same handful of checks every time. Here are 5 of them — run them on yourself before anyone else does:
>
> **1. Can you state the project in one sentence a non-expert trusts?** "We're building X, it produces Y, here's how it pays for itself." If it takes a paragraph, the readiness story isn't built yet.
>
> **2. Is your disclosure package assembled — not just *available*?** Financials, project specs, the draft official statement, sitting in one organized place. "We can pull that together" is not the same as "here it is." This is the #1 thing that stalls a first deal for quarters.
>
> **3. Do your covenants and projections survive a skeptic?** Not your numbers — a *skeptic's* read of your numbers. If you haven't had someone try to break the model, the underwriter will be the first, and that's the expensive place to find out.
>
> **4. Do you know which conduit issuer fits — and have you talked to them?** An IDA, a finance authority, a state HEFA. Eligibility is usually a one-meeting answer operators spend months guessing at.
>
> **5. Is there someone on your side who has run the gauntlet before?** First-timers pay a "first-deal tax" in time and rework. The check isn't "are you smart" — it's "do you have a map."
>
> Score yourself 0–5. Most operators who get told "you're two years out" are actually a 2 or 3 — months of organized work from fundable, not years. We built the system we use to close that gap on live engagements.
>
> Which of the 5 is your weakest right now? 👇
