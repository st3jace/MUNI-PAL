# Complete-drop checklist

The clock on anything — your own map, or our ten business days — starts when the drop is complete. This is what complete means.

## Gate

- [ ] The bonds have **closed**. Not authorized, not approved, not "cleared the board". Closed.
- [ ] You are the **obligated person**, or you act for one. Not a municipal entity or public authority.

## Documents (per series)

- [ ] Continuing Disclosure Agreement / Certificate / Undertaking
- [ ] Loan Agreement (or Financing Agreement, or Lease)
- [ ] Indenture of Trust (or Trust Agreement)
- [ ] Tax Regulatory Agreement (or Tax Certificate)
- [ ] Regulatory Agreement / Land Use Restriction Agreement (housing)
- [ ] Any supplement or amendment to the above
- [ ] The closing transcript index (the table of contents of the binder)
- [ ] For a refunded or amended deal: the refunding documents too

## People

- [ ] Bond counsel: firm, person, email
- [ ] Municipal advisor (if any): firm, person, email
- [ ] Dissemination agent (if any): firm, person, email
- [ ] The person on your side who owns the calendar today (even if the answer is "nobody")

## Evidence on hand

- [ ] Every annual report, audit, certificate, or notice you have already delivered, with the date it was sent and to whom
- [ ] Trustee statements, if you have them
- [ ] Anything a professional has already told you in writing about a deadline

## Facts

- [ ] Fiscal year end
- [ ] Series names and closing dates, copied from the documents
- [ ] Whether a new issuance is in contemplation in the next six months (if yes, say so first)

## What not to include

- Questions that need a legal answer. Write them down separately; they go to counsel, not into the drop.
