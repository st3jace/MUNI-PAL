## Executive Summary

Launch Shop's Bond Readiness Accelerator uses a graduated engagement path: two free steps that generate leads and demonstrate value, followed by three paid tiers that scale with deal complexity and client need. This document defines the paid tiers — scope, pricing, positioning, and market justification — so the team has an internal anchor before any client-facing pricing conversation.

---

## The Full Engagement Path

| Step | Name | Price | Purpose |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Market Intelligence Report (MIR) | Free | Lead generation. Automated sector snapshot showing where the borrower's deal fits in the market. No commitment required. |
| 2 | Readiness Scan | Free | Qualification. Self-serve 167-item readiness assessment against Muni-Pal benchmarks. Identifies gaps and generates a score. Positions the borrower to understand whether they need help. |
| 3 | **Bond Readiness Diagnostic** | **$15,000–$25,000** | Paid Tier 1. Detailed credit analysis and gap remediation roadmap. |
| 4 | **Standard Engagement** | **$40,000–$50,000** | Paid Tier 2. Diagnostic + active deal coordination + COI optimization. |
| 5 | **Bond Readiness Accelerator** | **$75,000+** | Paid Tier 3. Full pre-issuance support: gap remediation execution, UW benchmarking (platform self-serve), deal timeline optimization. |

The free steps (MIR + Scan) are lead-gen and qualification — they are not "tiers" in the pricing sense. The 3-tier pricing structure refers exclusively to the three paid engagements.

> **⚠ Counsel review required for free activities.** Board flagged that MA scrutiny is triggered by *activity*, not compensation. Free MIR delivery and Readiness Scan — even at $0 — involve analytical output that could be characterized as municipal advisory activity. Both must be included in the counsel briefing alongside the paid tier scope review.

## Tier 1: Bond Readiness Diagnostic ($15,000–$25,000)

### What the client gets

- **Comprehensive readiness assessment** against Muni-Pal's 167-item healthcare bond framework (hospital: 69 items, senior living: 72, FQHC bond: 68, FQHC CDFI: 38)
- **Credit memo** with DSCR analysis, debt capacity estimate, and peer comparison against Muni-Pal's healthcare corpus (866+ transactions)
- **Gap analysis report** identifying specific documentation, financial, and regulatory gaps that would delay or weaken an issuance
- **Comparable deal structures** — statistical summary of how borrowers of similar size and sub-sector have structured recent issuances (par, term, security, enhancement distributions). Presented as historical data, not recommendations.
- **Actionable timeline** with milestone estimates based on historical healthcare deal timelines

### What the client does NOT get

- Active deal coordination or document routing
- COI benchmarking or optimization
- Ongoing support beyond report delivery
- Advice on how to respond to rating agency questions, underwriter RFPs, or investor questions

### Deliverable

Written report (30–50 pages), delivered within 3 weeks of data receipt.

### Market justification

Traditional MA feasibility studies run $10,000–$50,000 depending on scope and deal size. At $15K–$25K, this tier is competitive with the low-to-mid range of feasibility study pricing while delivering a more data-driven, benchmark-rich product than a typical narrative feasibility report.

**Positioning note:** This is explicitly NOT a feasibility study (which involves financial projections and market analysis that may trigger MA registration). This is a readiness assessment — a gap analysis against historical benchmarks.

### Price driver (internal: willingness-to-pay based)

> **Internal note:** This is WTP-based pricing, not scope-based. The deliverable is identical across all par sizes — the same 167-item assessment, credit memo, gap analysis, comparable structures, and timeline. The price scales with the borrower's deal size (and therefore their ability and willingness to pay), not with incremental work. Do not justify higher prices to clients by implying "more work" — the justification is that the benchmarking dataset is more valuable to a larger issuer because the stakes are higher.

Scale to par amount:
- Sub-$25M deals: $15,000
- $25M–$75M deals: $20,000
- $75M+ deals: $25,000

## Tier 2: Standard Engagement ($40,000–$50,000)

### What the client gets

Everything in Tier 1, plus:

- **Active deal coordination** — document organization, categorization, and information request routing between borrower, underwriter, counsel, and MA (per engagement letter §1 scope)
- **COI benchmarking** — predicted cost of issuance line items based on Muni-Pal's comparable transaction dataset, presented as statistical ranges (not deal-specific projections)
- **Milestone tracking** — real-time deal progress against comparable timelines, with automated alerts when milestones lag historical benchmarks
- **Dedicated point of contact** at Launch Shop for the duration of the deal
- **Post-deal report** comparing actual outcomes to predictions (timeline, COI, time savings)

### What the client does NOT get

- Advice on deal structure, terms, timing, sale method, or choice of deal participants
- Rating agency presentation support
- Credit enhancement strategy
- Gap remediation execution (we identify gaps; the borrower and its advisors fix them)
- Advice on how to respond to rating agency questions, underwriter RFPs, or investor questions

### Deliverable

Ongoing support for the deal duration (typically 12–20 weeks), plus a written post-deal report within 30 days of close.

### Market justification

A full MA engagement for a mid-market healthcare deal runs $50,000–$150,000+ (typically $1.50–$2.50 per $1,000 of par). At $40K–$50K, this tier prices below a full MA engagement while delivering coordination and benchmarking value that complements — not replaces — the MA relationship. Key differentiator: the borrower retains its existing MA; Launch Shop handles the readiness and coordination layer the MA typically doesn't focus on.

**Internal reference only (do NOT use in client-facing materials):** Hilltop Securities and Raymond James price comparable advisory mandates at 10–25 bps on par. At $45K on a $50M deal, Launch Shop is at ~9 bps — below the floor of traditional advisory pricing. **Client-facing framing:** "a fraction of a municipal advisor fee" — positions Launch Shop in a different category (readiness/coordination) rather than as a cheaper version of the same service (which invites direct comparison and MA-classification risk).

### Price driver

Scale to deal complexity and duration:
- Single-series, straightforward refunding: $40,000
- New money with moderate complexity: $45,000
- Multi-series, new money, or complex credit structure: $50,000

## Tier 3: Bond Readiness Accelerator ($75,000+)

### What the client gets

Everything in Tier 2, plus:

- **Pre-issuance gap remediation roadmap execution support** — working alongside the borrower's team to close identified gaps before engaging underwriters and rating agencies
- ~~**Rating agency preparation support**~~ — **REMOVED pending counsel opinion.** Board determined this activity carries unacceptable MA-classification risk regardless of how narrowly scoped. Will not be offered until muni-finance counsel opines on whether document assembly and fact-checking against an MA-drafted narrative can be cleanly separated from advisory activity. If reinstated, scope will be limited to: *"Rating agency documentation support — assembly and organization of borrower-provided documents and fact-checking of data points against Muni-Pal benchmarks. Does not include drafting narratives, advising on presentation strategy, or coaching on agency questions."*
- **Underwriter selection benchmarking (platform self-serve)** — Muni-Pal provides historical underwriter performance data in the borrower's sub-sector (spread performance, deal volume, specialization) as a self-serve dataset within the platform. The borrower pulls this data independently; Launch Shop does not walk through the data, interpret it, or facilitate comparison. Presented as raw historical data, not recommendations.
- **Deal timeline optimization** — proactive coordination to compress the kickoff-to-pricing timeline based on historical comparable benchmarks
- **Extended post-deal analysis** — detailed comparison report with full COI line-item analysis, timeline milestone comparison, and recommendations for the borrower's next issuance

### What the client does NOT get

- Municipal advisory services as defined under Section 15B of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934
- Advice on terms, pricing, or sale method
- Fiduciary duties — the borrower's registered MA retains that role
- Post-closing surveillance or ongoing advisory
- Advice on how to respond to rating agency questions, underwriter RFPs, or investor questions

### Deliverable

Full engagement from pre-issuance through 30 days post-close (typically 16–24 weeks), plus extended post-deal report.

### Market justification

**Internal reference only (do NOT use in client-facing materials):** At $75K on a $50M deal, Launch Shop is at ~15 bps — within the range of traditional advisory pricing ($50K–$150K+ or 10–25 bps) but at the lower end. **Client-facing framing:** "a fraction of a municipal advisor fee" — the borrower gets readiness coordination and benchmarking data that complements (not replaces) the MA relationship. Never frame as "X bps on par" in client-facing materials; that positions Launch Shop in the same pricing category as MAs.

For larger deals ($100M+), pricing should scale: $75K is the floor, with $100K–$125K appropriate for deals above $100M par.

### Price driver

- $50M–$100M par: $75,000
- $100M–$200M par: $100,000
- $200M+ par: $125,000+

## Regulatory Positioning (All Tiers)

All tiers operate under the same regulatory framework:

> "Bond Readiness Accelerator is an educational and analytical service. It does not constitute municipal advisory services as defined under Section 15B of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or related SEC and MSRB rules."

**Key boundaries that must hold across all tiers:**

1. Launch Shop does NOT advise on deal structure, terms, timing, or sale method
2. Launch Shop does NOT recommend specific underwriters, counsel, or MAs — it presents data and benchmarks
3. Launch Shop does NOT replace the borrower's registered MA — it complements the MA relationship
4. All COI and pricing data is presented as "statistical ranges derived from historical comparables," never as deal-specific projections or recommendations
5. The engagement letter (§7) requires MA acknowledgment before the engagement becomes effective

**Tier 3 specifically** requires careful scoping: "gap remediation execution support" is the highest-risk remaining activity for MA classification. The engagement letter must narrowly scope this as organizational/coordination tasks (document assembly, milestone tracking) — never as strategic advice. **Rating agency preparation support has been removed entirely** pending counsel opinion (see Tier 3 section above).

**Counsel review status:** The engagement letter's §7 disclaimer and scope of services (§1) are flagged for muni-finance counsel review. The Section 15B(e)(4)(C) exclusion (unregistered persons working alongside a registered MA) may provide a cleaner regulatory path than IRMA. Counsel should opine before Tier 3 is offered to any client. **Additionally:** free MIR delivery and Readiness Scan must be included in the counsel briefing — activity triggers MA scrutiny regardless of compensation.

## Internal Pricing Anchors for Baseline Interview

Per the baseline interview script (Part 3), when Adventist is asked about pricing expectations:

- **MA fee anchor to deploy if needed:** "$1.50–$2.50 per $1,000 of par — so on a $200M deal, roughly $300K to $500K"
- **Internal interpretation framework:** If Adventist's response implies willingness in the $15K–$25K range → Tier 1 fit. $40K–$50K → Tier 2 fit. $75K+ → Tier 3 fit.
- **"Fraction of an MA fee"** is the correct buyer frame. If they land at 10–20% of the MA anchor, that maps to $30K–$100K — squarely in our Tier 2–3 range.

**Do NOT share tier names or specific prices with Adventist during the baseline interview.** The goal is to hear where they land unprompted, then map their response to our tiers internally.
